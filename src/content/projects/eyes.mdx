[Eyes](https://eyes.dev) is the analytics product we build at Pixel Perfect Agency. It's a powerful, privacy-friendly web and product analytics suite that turns complex product data into simple answers.

It goes further than page views: you can follow funnels and flows, retention and cohorts, custom events and users and segments, and it points out where people get stuck.

This website runs on Eyes too. Besides page views I track a few custom events with it, like searching the insights and opening a project.
